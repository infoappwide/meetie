How Can We Help?

Contact us about your account, dates or privacy.

On This PageContact SupportDiscovery and InstagramPrivate Relay and Account HelpSafety and Data Requests

Contact Support

Email infoappwide@gmail.com. Include the affected screen, approximate time and app version. Hide other people’s private details in screenshots. Do not send passwords, codes or identity documents with routine support messages. There is no website form; the email link opens your email app and does not send a message itself.

Discovery and Instagram

If discovery is empty, check this week’s availability and active meeting areas. There may be no profile with compatible mutual preferences and time. Instagram opens only after both users approve the same current proposal, and access may close on cancellation, blocking or restrictions.

Private Relay and Account Help

Apple private relay does not prevent you from contacting support. Explain the problem and your profile name; you may be guided through account verification without sharing Apple credentials. See Account Deletion or open a decision in Account Notices to appeal.

Safety and Data Requests

Use the date screen’s three-dot menu to report or block. If you cannot access the app, email support. This is not an emergency response service; contact local emergency services in immediate danger. See Personal Data Requests for statutory rights. General support has no guaranteed hourly response time.
