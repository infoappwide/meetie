From Sign-In to a Date

Core steps and ways to correct information.

On This Page1. Sign In and Create a Profile2. Availability and Discovery3. Planning4. Changes and Cancellation5. Notices, Language and Deletion

1. Sign In and Create a Profile

On iPhone, sign in with Apple. Enter your name, birth date, Instagram username and preferences, and add your own photo. Review errors before saving. Editable profile information and preferences can later be changed in Profile.

2. Availability and Discovery

Choose a day and a broad start/end range, then save. Explore compatible profiles after adding availability. Like or pass; mutual interest creates a match.

3. Planning

Open the match in My Matches. Choose a shared day/time; after time approval, select an eligible venue. Review the current date, time and place before final approval. One person’s approval alone does not confirm the date.

4. Changes and Cancellation

Use rescheduling or cancellation for a confirmed date. A changed time or venue needs fresh approval. Updating preferences or your calendar does not automatically cancel confirmed dates. Create a new proposal if the previous one expires.

5. Notices, Language and Deletion

Notifications lead to current plan information. Change language or delete your account in Profile → Account and Privacy. See Support if something goes wrong.
