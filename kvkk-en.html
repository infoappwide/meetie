1. Data Controller

The controller is Mert Küçükerdem. Location: Sarıyer/İstanbul, Türkiye. Contact: infoappwide@gmail.com. AppWide is a brand, not a separate company or controller. The location is not a complete postal delivery address; use email for current contact.

2. Data and Collection Methods

Data is obtained electronically through Apple sign-in responses, app forms and requests, user-selected photos, usage records and messages sent to support. It includes Apple identifiers and email/session details; profile name, date of birth, gender, Instagram username, dating and age preferences, meeting areas and availability; photos, reports and appeals; likes/passes, matches, proposal versions, approvals, cancellations, blocks, notification/read status, safety decisions and technical cleanup receipts. Providers may process IP addresses, request/error data, client details and IP-derived city/country data. meetie does not collect GPS location history, phone contacts, SMS content or the whole photo library.

3. Purposes and Legal-Basis Assessment

The proposed assessment is as follows; it is not evidence of an implemented consent mechanism or a completed legal determination. Apple identity/email, ordinary profile/age information and non-sensitive photo, availability and planning processing are assessed for necessity to establish or perform the service contract under Article 5(2)(c). Safety processing requires a specific legitimate-interest balancing assessment under Article 5(2)(f), or Article 5(2)(e) where necessary to establish, exercise or protect a right. Data-rights requests and authority requests rely on Article 5(2)(ç) only where an actual legal obligation applies, or Article 5(2)(e) for protection of rights. All bases do not automatically apply to every record. Gender and dating preferences together may enable inferences about sexual life or orientation. That sensitive processing needs a separate Article 6 assessment; ordinary contract or legitimate-interest grounds do not automatically cover it. General terms or Apple sign-in are not a substitute for any separately required explicit consent.

4. Recipients and Purposes

Eligible users receive the profile and shared-availability information shown in discovery; matches receive planning details. Instagram access requires both participants’ final approval of the current proposal. Supabase handles accounts, database, storage and server operations. Apple handles authentication and revocation on deletion. Google/Gmail handles support correspondence. The operator’s authorised reviewer accesses relevant reports, photos and appeals. Public authorities receive necessary information only under a concrete applicable obligation. Maps and Instagram are external services contacted by your device when opened. meetie does not send venues reservations or attendee lists.

5. International Processing

The production Supabase project is in Frankfurt. Apple and Gmail are separate processing routes. When this information website is served through GitHub Pages, GitHub is also a service provider for website access. Serving Türkiye does not mean data is stored only in Türkiye. Region selection does not establish the location of every support or subprocessor operation. A meetie-specific Article 9 transfer mechanism has not yet been documented; no executed Turkish standard contract, completed notification or applicable adequacy decision is represented as being in place. Accepting general terms does not resolve that outstanding assessment.

6. Retention and Erasure

Active account data supports the service. Deletion removes or cleans the profile, private information, preferences, availability, associated matching/planning access and photos. Technical deletion receipts, upload records, Apple revocation retries and certain safety records have separate lifecycles. Past availability enters daily cleanup 90 days after ending. Completed deletion receipts and cleaned upload receipts with known cleanup timestamps enter daily cleanup after 30 days. Pending operations or necessary file/revocation dependencies retain the receipt. Older upload receipts without a cleanup date are excluded from this automatic process. Closed routine support email is reviewed after 90 days, not automatically deleted. These periods do not apply to all safety records, provider logs, backups or caches; no uniform deletion deadline for those categories has been verified.

7. Your Rights

You may learn whether your data is processed, request information about processing and its purpose, ask whether it is used appropriately and learn domestic or foreign recipients. You may request correction, erasure or destruction where statutory conditions apply and notification of those actions to recipients. You may object to an adverse result arising solely from automated analysis and seek compensation for damage caused by unlawful processing.

8. Requests

Write to infoappwide@gmail.com with the subject “meetie — Personal Data Request”. Describe the request and include only the information needed to identify your account. Do not send your Apple password or sign-in code. Account ownership may need proportionate verification. For a formal request sent from an ordinary email address, use the address previously provided to and recorded by the controller. If you use Apple private relay or cannot use that address, contact support for verification guidance; the request will not be automatically rejected solely because the sender differs. This does not represent an unimplemented channel as an approved formal method. Statutory written/electronic methods remain available under applicable law. Valid requests are handled as soon as appropriate and within 30 days; this is not a 24/7 support promise. Requests are generally free, subject to any permitted Board tariff for additional costs. If a response is refused or inadequate, or no timely response arrives, statutory complaint rights remain available, normally within 30 days of learning the response and in all cases within 60 days of the initial request.
