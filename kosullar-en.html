3. Matches and Dates

Mutual likes create a match. A date is confirmed only after both participants finally approve the same current time and venue. Changes require new approval. The expiry shown on the proposal controls; an expired proposal cannot be approved. Venue selection follows the eligible participant shown in the app, and both retain final approval. Either participant may cancel. Changing preferences or availability does not automatically cancel a confirmed date; use rescheduling or cancellation.

4. Instagram and External Services
