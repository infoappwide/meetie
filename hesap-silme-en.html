Account and Data Deletion

You do not need to wait for moderator approval.

On This PageDelete in the AppDeleted Data and Closed AccessSeparate LifecyclesApple ConnectionCannot Access Your Account?

Delete in the App

Open Profile → Account and Privacy → Delete My Account. Read and confirm the warning; Apple authentication may appear. Successful deletion returns you to sign-in. Restricted accounts also have deletion access. On a connection error, do not assume success; use the offered retry. A deleted profile cannot be restored. Signing up again with the same Apple account does not restore it.

Deleted Data and Closed Access

Account and profile data, private profile information, preferences, availability and associated like/match/date/approval and Instagram access are cleaned up. Profile photo storage cleanup is performed. Other users’ own accounts remain; access to shared plans closes.

Separate Lifecycles

Deletion/upload cleanup receipts, Apple revocation retries and certain safety records may remain separately. Some safety records have text and deleted-user links removed while category, date or another participant’s association remains; they are not described as fully anonymous. See Retention and Erasure for the 90/30-day cleanup scope. Old short-lived photo links, caches, provider logs and backups may not disappear simultaneously. Previously copied Instagram information cannot be recalled.

Apple Connection

Apple access revocation is attempted alongside deletion and may be retried through a queue. meetie account deletion does not wait for that retry. If instructed by the app, revoke meetie in iPhone Settings → Apple Account → Sign in with Apple.

Cannot Access Your Account?

Email infoappwide@gmail.com with “meetie — Account Deletion”. Describe the account and access problem without passwords or Apple codes. Proportionate ownership verification protects against deleting another person’s account. This website does not contain an automatic deletion form.
