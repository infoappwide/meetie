Adults and Authentic Profiles

meetie is for adults aged 18 and over. Use accurate information and photos you have the right to use. Apple sign-in is not identity or background verification.

Respect Boundaries

Harassment, threats, hate, coercion, fraud, requests for money, stalking and disclosing private information without permission are unacceptable. Matching, revealing Instagram or approving a date is not unlimited consent to physical contact or continued communication. You may end contact or cancel a date.

Reporting and Review

Use report/block tools or email infoappwide@gmail.com. An authorised reviewer considers reports; a report does not automatically impose a penalty. Eligible warnings, photo removals and restrictions can be appealed. Access to legal authorities remains available.

Before Meeting

Choose a public place for a first date and share your plan with someone you trust. Do not accept suspicious requests for money. meetie cannot guarantee another person’s behaviour. Support is not an emergency service.
