Privacy Policy

Know which information is used and who can see it.

1. Operator and Contact

meetie is operated by Mert Küçükerdem, based in Sarıyer/İstanbul, Türkiye. AppWide is a brand name. Contact infoappwide@gmail.com with privacy questions. See the Personal Data Notice for processing purposes, proposed legal bases and rights.

2. Account and Profile

Sign in with Apple provides an Apple account identifier and an email address, which may be a private relay address. We do not receive your Apple password. The first release does not use phone or SMS sign-in. Your name, date of birth, gender, profile photo, Instagram username, dating preferences, preferred ages and meeting areas are used for your account and discovery. Age is calculated from your date of birth; your full date of birth is not shown to other users.

3. Photos, Areas and Availability

Only the profile photo you choose and save is uploaded; your entire photo library is not scanned. Current photo uploads are converted to JPEG on the device and technical metadata such as EXIF/GPS is removed. This does not remove visible information in the picture or establish that older uploads were processed in the same way. There is no facial recognition or identity-verification badge. You choose meeting areas yourself; meetie does not track live GPS location. Saved availability is used to find shared times. Other users see relevant shared availability and date information, rather than your entire calendar.

4. Instagram Access

You can view and change your own username in your profile. The other person can access it only after both of you finally approve the same current proposal. Changed times or venues require new approval. Cancellation, blocking, account deletion or safety restrictions can close access. Information already seen or copied cannot be taken back from another person or device. A person whose access remains valid sees your updated username. meetie does not verify ownership of an Instagram account.

5. Users and Providers

Eligible users can see your name, age, photo and the area/shared-time information shown in discovery. A match can see planning status. Email addresses, full birth dates and internal report-review notes are not public profile fields. Apple provides sign-in; Supabase provides authentication, database, server functions and photo storage, with the production project in Frankfurt. Support email uses Gmail. Provider requests and logs can contain IP addresses, client information and IP-derived city/country information. Venue photos may be loaded from external servers. Opening Maps or Instagram sends a request to the external service, whose own terms apply. An EU project region does not mean all provider processing occurs in that region or itself establish a Turkish international-transfer mechanism.

6. Safety and Moderation

Likes, passes, matches, proposals and versions, confirmations, cancellations, blocks, notifications, reports and appeals support the service. An authorised reviewer may review relevant reports and photos. The reporter’s identity and internal review notes are not disclosed to the reported person. Session and server-side access controls are used; they do not guarantee perfect security or verify every user’s real identity.

7. Retention and Deletion

Delete your account in Profile → Account and Privacy → Delete My Account, without moderator approval. See Account and Data Deletion. Past availability enters daily cleanup 90 days after its end. Completed account-deletion receipts and upload receipts with a known cleanup date enter cleanup after 30 days, subject to outstanding processing dependencies. Closed routine support emails are reviewed after 90 days; this is not automatic email deletion. Some technical retry and reduced-linkage safety records may remain. Provider logs, backups and caches have separate lifecycles; simultaneous deletion of every copy is not promised.

8. Your Rights

See the rights and requests sections to request information, corrections, deletion where applicable and exercise other rights. Uninstalling the app does not delete your account.

9. Changes

The version date is shown on this page. Changes in processing practices require corresponding notice updates. Reading this policy is not blanket consent to all processing or marketing.
